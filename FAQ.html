Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Is Money Mate free?

Yes! The basic version of Money Mate is completely free to use. In the future, we may introduce premium features for advanced analytics and custom category management, but core tracking will always be free.

Which languages does it understand?

Money Mate is powered by advanced LLMs (like Gemini/Claude) so it understands English, Hindi, Hinglish, and many other regional variations perfectly. "Bhai 500 ka petrol dalwaya" works just as well as "Spent $6 on gas".

Can I export my data?

Absolutely. Your data belongs to you. You can export all your transactions into a clean CSV file at any time by typing /export in the chat.

How does the Ledger feature work?

When you tell the bot that you lent money to someone or paid for someone else (e.g., "Gave 1000 to Aman"), it adds that to your ledger as an amount owed to you. You can type /ledger anytime to see who owes you what. When they pay you back, just say "Aman paid me 1000 back".

Do I need to download another app?

No! That's the beauty of Money Mate. It lives inside Telegram. If you have Telegram installed on your phone or desktop, you already have Money Mate.
