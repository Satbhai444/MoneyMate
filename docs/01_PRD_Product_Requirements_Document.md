# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Project Overview
**Product Name:** Money Mate
**Platform:** Telegram Bot (with a web-based landing page & documentation)
**Tagline:** Your money, one chat away. AI Native Expense Tracking built for Telegram.

## 2. Problem Statement
Most expense tracking applications require users to install a heavy app, navigate through complex UI menus, and manually fill out forms (Amount, Category, Date, Note) just to log a simple transaction. People often forget to log expenses or track IOUs because the process involves too much friction.

## 3. Solution
Money Mate is an AI-powered expense tracker that lives directly inside Telegram. It removes friction by allowing users to log expenses, incomes, and IOUs just by chatting normally using text or voice. The AI automatically parses natural language (including Hindi, Hinglish, and English) and structures the data automatically.

## 4. Target Audience
- College students managing allowances and shared expenses.
- Young professionals living in shared apartments.
- Freelancers looking for a quick way to track petty cash.
- Anyone who finds traditional finance apps too complicated.

## 5. Key Features
- **Natural Language Input:** "Chai aur samosa 60 rupaye", "Salary aayi 45k".
- **Multi-lingual Support:** Understands English, Hindi, and Hinglish natively.
- **IOU Ledger:** Tracks who owes you money and who you owe ("Rahul ko 500 diye").
- **Voice Support:** Users can send voice notes to log expenses on the go.
- **Zero App Install:** Works completely via Telegram.
- **Automated Summaries:** "Show my monthly report" generates an instant breakdown.

## 6. Success Metrics
- **User Activation:** Percentage of users who log more than 5 expenses in their first week.
- **Retention:** Monthly Active Users (MAU) retaining their usage month-over-month.
- **NLP Accuracy:** Percentage of messages parsed correctly without manual user correction.
